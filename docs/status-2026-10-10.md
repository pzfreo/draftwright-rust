## Thin slice S4 part A: render split, bounds and PMI caches

- `model::render_geometry` produces the geometry layer; `render_annotations` assembles it with
  sheet annotations. `render_with` and fresh `render` use those same paths. `render_item` emits
  independent dimensions/callouts and PMI frames/datums/labels, including their measured ink,
  text and provenance; appending fragments rebases local text-run indices.
- `Projections` retains HLR by direction/up/cut and the latest placed curves and visible bounds
  by scale/centre/origin. A new source binding clears the caches. View-bounds and projection
  counters measure actual work. Geometry arithmetic, draw order and placement remain unchanged.
- `pmi::Reader` retains the latest report per immutable part and PMI digest. Material, threads
  and frames consume that one report. Repeated bytes, empty PMI and refused PMI do not read
  again; changing material replaces the report after one reading attempt. The standalone
  analysis path already used one shared report; the new reader makes reuse explicit for callers
  keeping an Analysis between edits.
- `tests/render_caches.rs`: 12 tests. Eight omit/tolerance tests (two on each slice part) compare
  kept and fresh SVG, DXF and PDF bytes, sheet bounds, annotations and problems, and assert no
  HLR or bounds recomputation for unchanged views. All eight failed before the cache change
  with actual counters instrumented into the old renderer: it measured every view again.

  part replacement, single-read material/thread/frame consumers plus changed material, and
  empty/refused PMI replacement. Before the new APIs these four failed to compile because the
