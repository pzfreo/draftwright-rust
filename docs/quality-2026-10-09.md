Ink 486 to 449. By code (all 30 parts): `annotation_ink_overlap` 30 to 16,
`annotation_outside_area` 280 to 256, `annotation_leader_crossing` 124 to 124 (some moved, see
below), `annotation_over_view` 15 to 16, `annotation_line_over_view` 37 to 37. Every other
column is unchanged but `off_sheet` (ftc09 21 to 20). What changed, all in the callout leaders:

- **Text is protected, and each pair counts once.** A leader's crossings are counted per other
  item met, as `collisions` reports them (one problem per pair), not per segment; between as many
  crossings the one that touches text (a line through the label, the label on a label, a leader
  or an arrowhead's flare into a label) loses (draftwright protects text both ways,
  `annotation_text_ink_clear`). This is most of the `annotation_ink_overlap` fall (ctc02 −4,
  ftc08 −4, ctc04, ctc03, ftc10 −2 each); on ftc08, ftc10 and ctc04 a label taken off a line
  costs its leader a crossing instead (+4, +2, +1).
- **Ink inside the view's area is preferred** (below crossings, above length): callouts that
  stood a few millimetres past their view's bands move in (`annotation_outside_area` −28 over 17
  parts; ctc03's +4 is below). On ftc09 the one callout whose every place is blocked now lies over
  its own view rather than off the sheet (`annotation_over_view` +1, `annotation_off_sheet` −1).
- **ISSUE-1187-a, the clear-route sweep:** after the joint assignment a callout still blocked or
  crossing tries draftwright's `_clear_routes` sweep (its leader leaving its sites radially in 64
  directions at ten reaches) and takes the best route better than its own that meets no other
  leader; `dims::clear_routes` measures it, and on ctc04 every retained crossing has no clear
  route (`tests/issue_regressions.rs`). On these parts it finds few moves: the crossings left are
  leaders out of views whose location ladders' extension lines and dimension lines fence them in
  (Python draftwright's drawings of ctc04, ftc08 and ftc09 cross the same way, ctc04 and ftc09
  with long leaders across the whole view).

**One part is worse in the ink column: ctc03, 21 to 23.** Its first plan (A3 at 1:5) now has one
hard layout finding where it had three, and A2 at 1:5 is now valid and complete (it had one), so
the recovery takes A2 (`layout_replanned`), as it should: the A2 sheet has no text overlap (ink
overlaps 2 to 0, label overlaps 1 to 0) but four more linear dimensions past their bands
(`annotation_outside_area`, info), the layout's band estimates on the larger sheet. Verdict:
rust-correct for this stream (a sheet with no hard finding is taken over one with one); the band
residual is the layout's (stream d-quality-view-overlap).

**Not reached by this stream**, and why: 228 of the 256 `annotation_outside_area` and all the
`annotation_line_over_view` and linear `annotation_over_view` are linear dimensions in lanes past
bands that are draftwright's estimates (`layout::Strips::reserve` reserves the port's lanes for
the side view only; the front's right ladder runs across the side view on ftc09, ftc10, cgb245),
which is the layout's (d-quality-view-overlap). A fewest-conflicts fallback for linear labels
along their lines (draftwright's `_InkConflictState.solve` descent) was tried and changed nothing
on these parts, so it was not kept.
