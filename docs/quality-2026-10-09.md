## After Quality 3 (overlapping views), 2026-10-09

(`layout::geometry`) now reserves every view's bands when it arranges the sheet:

- the isometric's rectangle keeps clear of the principal views' full blocks, bands included, as
  well as draftwright's DIM_PAD silhouettes. draftwright's estimate packs the iso around the
  silhouettes alone because its measure-and-repack pass then moves it clear of the measured blocks

  rectangle lying on a view's bands;

  section), not DIM_PAD, which lay 10 mm over the side's band (6 pairs);
- neighbouring blocks abut exactly: rounding had them overlap by about 3e-14 mm (3 pairs, ctc05 and

  below the views, the side's is 21 mm), the row is lifted clear if the sheet has the height, and
