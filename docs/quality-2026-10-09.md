## After Quality 3 (overlapping views), 2026-10-09

Branch `r/d-quality-view-overlap`, rebased on origin/main 523cfe7 (page/scale escalation and
repeated dimensions merged; same tool, parts and command, `plan` now applying the recovery). The
layout (`layout::geometry`) now reserves every view's bands when it arranges the sheet:

- the isometric's rectangle keeps clear of the principal views' full blocks, bands included, as
  well as draftwright's DIM_PAD silhouettes. draftwright's estimate packs the iso around the
  silhouettes alone because its measure-and-repack pass then moves it clear of the measured blocks
  (#121); the port plans from the estimate alone, so most overlapping pairs were the iso's
  rectangle lying on a view's bands. A repack (measured blocks given) still packs as draftwright's;
- a section block's left band is its 10 mm gap to the side's band (draftwright's row budget for a
  section), not DIM_PAD, which lay 10 mm over the side's band;
- neighbouring blocks abut exactly: rounding had them overlap by about 3e-14 mm (ctc05, 10047);
- when a band would lie on the title block (10020: draftwright's row verdict assumes a DIM_PAD band
  below the views, the side's is 21 mm), the row is lifted clear if the sheet has the height, and
  otherwise the layout does not fit (`fits` and `auto_fits` false), so the choice moves on. The
  lift applies on the repack path too, and never widens the row's right limit past the title
  block (draftwright judges that on the unlifted row): an earlier version did, and string_post on
  a named A4 went to 1:5000 through the step-count convergence instead of draftwright's 1:1.

Every recorded `choose_scale` call still gives draftwright's choice; the departures from
draftwright's recorded estimate (isometric rectangles, the lifted row) are listed with the port's
values and verdicts in `tests/fixtures/known_layout.json`.
